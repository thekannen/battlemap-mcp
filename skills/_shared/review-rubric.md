# Visual review rubric

## Brief fit

Can a viewer identify the requested event, mood and primary location at first glance?

## Composition

Does the focal event have priority, a readable approach and quiet counterspace?

## Material depth

Do floor, wall, terrain, water and light transitions form separate planes?

## Believability

Do purposeful prop clusters vary without blocking routes?

## Playability

At token scale, are entrances, cover, hazards, routes and requested VTT semantics clear?
