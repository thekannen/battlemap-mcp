## Custom Snap Mod, by Lievven

`server/battlemap_mcp/snapping.py` ports the snapping math of Lievven's
Custom Snap Mod (`snappy_mod.gd`), so that coordinates the assistant sends
land where the user's own cursor would snap them.
Source: https://github.com/Lievven/Snappy-Mod
